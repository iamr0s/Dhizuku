Ere fiyelen, tana i ejen i juri be gūwa fiyelen de dende ombi.
utai, ere fiyelen tana i ejen i juri be fiyelen sasa hūlame ombi.

Temgetu, ere juri emu fiyelen teile de dende ombi,
tuttu oci, Dhizuku si tana i ejen i juri be gūwa fiyelen de dende mutere.

Iliburengge ja (misal: Shizuku, QR kod).
Biretei bai <a href="https://github.com/iamr0s/Dhizuku/discussions/19">tacibun</a> be hūla.

Baitalan misal, juwe fiyelen i tana ejen juri be emu erin de aisilame bio.

Oiyo, Nihon de, Aurora Store i Test DPC be juwe fiyelen i tana ejen juri de aisilame ombi,
emu fiyelen de teile akū.

Fiyelen i weilede Dhizuku be alime gairakū seme,
Dhizuku-API-Xposed i asargan be baitalafi, Dhizuku be kenehunjeme ombi.
