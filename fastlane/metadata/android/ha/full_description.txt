Wannan app ɗin na iya ba da ayyuka daga mai na'urar.
Wato wannan app ɗin yana aiki azaman mai shiga tsakani don kiran ayyuka daga mai na'urar.

A al'ada, ana iya wakilta wannan ikon zuwa app guda ɗaya kawai, amma Dhizuku yana ba ku damar wakilta cikakken ikon mai na'urar ga wasu apps.

Yana da sauƙin saita (misali: Shizuku, lambar QR).
Don ƙarin bayani, da fatan za a duba <a href="https://github.com/iamr0s/Dhizuku/discussions/19">tutorial</a>.

Misalin amfani, yana da kyau lokacin da kuke buƙatar tallafawa izini biyu ko fiye da masu mallakar na'ura a lokaci guda.

Musamman, a cikin Japan, kuna buƙatar tallafawa duka Aurora Store da Gwaji DPC akan na'urar da ke da iyakancewar ayyuka.

Ko da mai haɓaka app baya tallafawa Dhizuku, kuna iya tilasta goyan baya ga Dhizuku ta amfani da ƙirar Dhizuku-API-Xposed.
